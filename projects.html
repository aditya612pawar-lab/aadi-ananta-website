Overview

About the Project

Aadi Ananta Shiloshi is a thoughtfully designed nature and wellness-focused development that brings together peaceful living, recreation, wellness and modern comforts in a natural environment.

The project is designed around the idea of creating a lifestyle where residents can reconnect with nature while enjoying thoughtfully planned spaces and amenities. From lush green surroundings and organic farming to wellness facilities, meditation, yoga, swimming, horse riding and community spaces, Aadi Ananta combines nature with a comfortable modern lifestyle.

The project focuses on creating an environment where architecture and nature work together through thoughtfully planned residential spaces, landscaped areas, wellness facilities, recreational activities and agricultural spaces.
